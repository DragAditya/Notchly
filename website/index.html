01The interface

A quieter way to use Windows.

Notchly is not a dashboard wearing a dark theme. It is one surface that lives at the top of the screen and opens only when you need it.

It has weight, depth, a rim that catches the light, and motion that follows the content instead of fighting it.

03Screenshot flow

Keep it. Delete it. Or let it wait.

The newest screenshot opens in the notch with a live deletion timer. Older shots wait in the Shelf, each with its own countdown.

  • Keep saves the file and adds it to the Shelf.
  • Delete removes it immediately.
  • Do nothing and it folds back into the Shelf.

04Notch lock

Lock it down. Nothing gets through.

Ctrl+Alt+L freezes the notch in idle. Music, notifications, required actions, peeks and global opens cannot expand it.

Try it. The notch will shake, flash red, and refuse to open.

05Make it yours

It should feel like your Windows.

A black notch or a floating Windows-style pill. Gradient rims, thickness, glow, radius, scale, blur, density and motion — all in a real settings window.
